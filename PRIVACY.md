# Privacy Policy

**SweatShift**  
Effective date: September 1, 2026

SweatShift is a workout timer app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Personal data

SweatShift does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Data on your device

Workout selections and timer activity remain on your device. They are not uploaded to a server or sent to anyone else.

## What the app does not include

SweatShift has no:

- Accounts or sign-in
- Advertising
- Analytics
- Tracking
- In-app purchases

## Children

SweatShift does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, the updated policy will be posted in this file.
