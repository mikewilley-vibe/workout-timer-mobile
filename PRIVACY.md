# SweatShift Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

SweatShift does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Workout selections and timer activity remain on the user’s device. SweatShift does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or online data storage.

SweatShift does not knowingly collect information from children.

If SweatShift’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated.

For questions about this privacy policy, please use the Issues section of the SweatShift GitHub repository:

https://github.com/mikewilley-vibe/workout-timer-mobile/issues
